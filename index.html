Overview

Dhoolpet, in Hyderabad's old city, is where many of the city's Ganesh idols are made. The film moves through the workshops as the idols are finished — eyes being painted, trunks and ornaments completed, figures many times human height waiting under tarpaulin.

Approach

Scale first, then detail: wide frames of idols lined up under the shed roof, then close passes across eyes, trunks and painted jewellery. The title sits low in frame so the faces stay clean.

SC 04 · Photo series · Festival portraiture · 21.08.2026

Goddess Night

Late at night, performers dressed as the goddess move through the crowd — crowns of beaten gold, faces painted in turmeric, vermilion and black. Long lens, tight crops, nothing in the background but night. Three of the four frames sit between 100 and 125mm: close enough to fill the frame with a face, far enough to stay out of the performers' way. The last switches wide for the full figure.

SC 05 · Photo series · Motorsport · 24.03.2026

Dust & Throttle

Riders in full gear and cars sliding on loose earth, with a crowd pressed along the barrier. Ninety minutes of runs, start to finish. Most frames at 120mm or longer from trackside. The long lens flattens the dust into a warm backdrop so each rider reads cleanly; the final runs are graded to monochrome, reducing the cars to shape and texture.

SC 06 · Photo series · Live music · 2025 — 2026

Under the Rig

Two live nights: a band on a smoke-filled stage under twin spotlights, and a concert washed in violet and teal haze. Timing over everything — waiting for the beam to land, using haze for depth and backlight for shape. Shot from the floor at up to 120mm.

Meet the creator

Kishore

Photographer and videographer, Hyderabad. An emerging visual storyteller drawn to the hardest light in the city.

Introduction

Kishore photographs and films the city's biggest moments from inside the crowd — festival processions after midnight, Dhoolpet's idol workshops, race days on a dirt track, college stages and live music under haze.

His films are short and built for the phone screen: vertical reels and compact documentaries with titles and captions cut into the edit. His photographs sit beside them — the same nights, held still.

He is early in his career and looking for the next step: a full-time role in a studio, agency or media team, freelance event work, and brand collaborations.

Approach

Wait for the light, stay close to the people, and let the place tell the story.
